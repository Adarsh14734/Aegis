=== 3. NETWORK denials are NOT usefully observable ===
a) a domain refusal never reaches the kernel at all:
   'curl https://evil.xyz/' returned http=000, and the whole session
   produced 1 network-outbound line — which was nc's, not curl's.
   ASRT refuses domains in its loopback proxy; the kernel never sees it.
b) a raw-socket denial does reach the kernel, but with no host:
   2026-08-19 16:07:01.452 E  kernel[0:2232b] (Sandbox) Sandbox: nc(7936) deny(1) network-outbound remote:*:443
   ^ 'remote:*:443'. Port only. The hostname is not recoverable.
   => Aegis records no host for a blocked request, because it cannot see one.
